# 24/7 AI Receptionist (VoxDesk)

A 24/7 AI-powered voice receptionist for inbound and outbound calls. It answers
calls, qualifies leads, books appointments, answers FAQs, and routes callers with
natural, real-time conversation — with industry templates, multi-language support,
custom voice cloning, a public API, and a no-code agent model.

> **Status:** This repository is being bootstrapped. A working prototype and the
> product documents currently live in the development workspace and are being
> imported here. See the guide below for the exact, ordered process.

## What the platform will do

- Answer inbound calls 24/7 and place approved outbound calls.
- Natural, low-latency, human-like conversation with interruption handling.
- 20+ industry templates and 50+ language support.
- Lead capture and qualification.
- Appointment booking with calendar integrations.
- FAQ answering grounded in a per-business knowledge base.
- Intelligent routing, transfer, and after-hours behavior.
- Instant phone-number provisioning.
- Custom voice cloning (consent-verified).
- Public REST API and signed webhooks.
- Multi-tenant accounts, billing, and analytics.
